# 01 · Hackathon facts

## The event

- **AI × Robotics Hackathon II**, HackLab Robotics in collaboration with **BRISA** (Berlin Robotics & Intelligent Systems Association).
- **21–22 November 2026**, CIC Berlin, Lohmühlenstraße 65. 48 hours.
- 1,100+ registrations, 200 builders selected. €10,000+ in prizes (cash, hardware, credits).
- Hardware on site: robot arms, robot sets and a Unitree Go2 EDU. Technology partners give free credits.

We are in the **NormaCore** track. The robot is NormaCore's ElRobot running Station.

## Judging

No criteria were published. Expect the usual: it works live, it uses the sponsor's technology in depth, it is creative and useful, and it is presented clearly. Sponsor judges tend to pick the project they would want to show off themselves.

**Action at the opening (BENCH):** ask the NormaCore team directly what they want to see in a winning project, and whether they ship any Claude/MCP integration of their own.
